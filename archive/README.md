## Pilot interpretation

Small pilots suggested that synthesis format, unstated metric conventions,
paraphrased narrative output, and benchmark question quality could dominate apparent
accuracy. These observations motivated stricter contracts and deterministic
quantitative execution; they do not establish broad Gemma-versus-Qwen rankings.

The proposed full factorial matrix and 500-case official baseline remain uncompleted.
A cancelled candidate build is not a published benchmark. The
[Companies House findings](../docs/companies-house-findings.md) preserve bounded
inspection results with explicit denominators and provenance.

## Preservation policy

Archives exclude logs, private identities, deployment paths, raw completions, review
decisions and unfinished outputs. Original private artifacts are preserved separately.
Earlier Git commits remain accessible; this cleanup does not rewrite history.

The deployment-specific exploratory authorization schema has a separately versioned
portable successor (`v2.4p.1`). Other historical public contract snapshots remain intact.
