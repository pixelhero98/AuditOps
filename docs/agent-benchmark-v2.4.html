Artifact lifecycle

  1. draft-agent-benchmark-v24 creates a content-addressed candidate and evaluator-only JSON review packet. It cannot create a benchmark manifest.
  2. render-agent-benchmark-review-v24 renders the 200 narrative cases, top-five evidence, answer windows/anchors, and refusal scope into self-contained HTML.
  3. prepare-agent-benchmark-review-bundle-v24 creates a checksum-bound offline directory containing the exact HTML, decision templates, all 20 filing documents, and searchable full-scope text. Standard refusals bind canonical_scope.txt; CAM refusals bind the canonical scope plus the recorded raw-CAM supplement. The bundle is evaluator-only and carries no approval.
  4. A primary human reviews all 200 cases. A second human independently reviews the frozen 40-case sample. A disagreement forces both reviewers to re-review the complete affected subtype.
  5. approve-agent-benchmark-v24 verifies different human identities, reviewer roles, exact memberships, boolean checks, rejection comments, packet/source hashes, and subtype-wide adjudication. Codex, templates, or another model cannot be a reviewer.
  6. freeze-agent-benchmark-v24 publishes only an approved candidate. Review and gold artifacts remain evaluator-only.

Narrative gold

NarrativeGoldV2.4 is evaluator-only. An answer may match any approved answer set, but every selected exact quote must remain inside its evidence-bound support window (maximum 1,000 characters) and contain all required semantic anchors. Preferred wording is diagnostic only. Refusals carry a human-reviewed filing scope and causal negative type.

Candidate construction uses the canonical narrative chunks for footnote, accounting-policy, and opinion tasks. For CAM tasks only, it also reads the matching frozen raw filing ZIP and recovers sentence-bounded CAM text when table layout omitted the matter body from canonical chunks. The corpus ZIP checksum, HTML-member checksum, extraction-policy version, section hash, and sentence count are recorded in the source manifest. This supplement is ranked before support is selected and never consults gold.

Human-review separation

The primary template contains all 200 narrative task IDs. The secondary template contains only the deterministic 40-case sample and must be completed without access to the primary decisions. Templates fail closed as REJECT, use a deliberately invalid reviewer identity, and remain inside the read-only bundle; reviewers copy them to a separate decisions directory before editing.

Top-five evidence can establish positive support but cannot prove absence. Every refusal is therefore mapped to the exact complete text scope and SHA-256 used during construction. Reviewers search the bound scope for the stated claim and reasonable synonyms. A real defect requires a new candidate; adjudication cannot repair or waive an immutable candidate defect.

Go/no-go sequence

The frozen 50-case gate contains 20 footnote, 10 accounting-policy, 10 opinion-language, and 10 CAM cases, balanced by answerability, with all 20 issuers represented at least twice. Exact Gemma requests must first pass the 14,336-token preflight. Technical evaluation then requires 50 typed results and zero malformed stopped outputs, cap hits, context/infrastructure/integrity failures, unsupported releases, invalid citations, arithmetic discrepancies, verifier bypasses, or second repairs.

A technical pass is not authorization. A human must confirm that every released quote answers its question and that no benchmark defect was found. A separate explicit GO authorization bound to the gate metrics is required before the full 500-case runner will start.

Interpretation boundary
